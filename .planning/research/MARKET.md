# MARKET — Tarot mobile 2024–2026

- **Casual** — curiosità, condivisione social, 1–2 sessioni/settimana
- **Daily habit** — arcano del giorno, notifiche, streak
- **Spiritual seekers** — journaling, intenzione, profondità interpretativa
- **Learners** — enciclopedia arcana, reversed, spread didattici

## Trend
- AI interpretations (on-device privacy angle)
- Intention/question-based readings
- Shareable visual cards (Instagram/TikTok)
- Local journal without account
- Widget + notifications for retention
